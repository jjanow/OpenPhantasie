# OpenPhantasie 1.3.0

**No game files are included.** As before, OpenPhantasie builds each game from your own copy on first start. Installed
copies update themselves (Options → General → UPDATE NOW, or the check at start).

## Downloads

- Windows: `OpenPhantasie-1.3.0-win-x64.zip`: unzip and run `OpenPhantasie.exe`
- Linux: `OpenPhantasie-1.3.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`: unpack and run `OpenPhantasie/OpenPhantasie`
- macOS: `OpenPhantasie-1.3.0-osx-arm64.tar.gz` (Apple silicon) or `-osx-x64.tar.gz` (Intel): unpack and open
  `OpenPhantasie.app` (not notarised: right-click → Open the first time)

## New

- **Commodore 64 Phantasie II and III** import from the C64 disks (`.d64` / `.g64`): II v1.2, III v1.0 and v1.2.
  They use the C64's text with the numbers of the version the remaster follows (the ST's for II, DOS's for III).
  Phantasie III starts with the C64's six ready-made characters in the Guild.
- **C64 art for Phantasie I, II and III:** monsters with their attack frames, portraits, map and dungeon tiles. I and
  II also get the C64's found-treasure and dungeon pictures and its ending and party-wipe screens, animated as on the
  C64. A C64-only copy now plays with pictures.
- **C64 sound:** fights, swimming, dungeon events, I's ring tune, the end screens and the key click, played through a
  model of the C64's SID chip. Phantasie III's C64 copy uses its own wording.
- **Apple II Phantasie I** imports from the Apple II disks (`.dsk` or `.woz`) as a playable game with the Apple's
  text and its own art (monsters, portraits, map tiles, title picture). Fights play on the Apple's combat field when
  ORIGINAL SCREENS is on, and the party wipe and Zeus's ending play as on the Apple, thunder included.
- **Apple II combat fields** for Phantasie II and III too (II's thrown rock, III's monster plot).

## Changed: Phantasie I's ending

Beating the Black Lord now gives what the Apple II version gives, in every version of I: three diamonds among the found
items, a new divine spell, DIVINE REVIVAL (cast in a dungeon, it brings back members whose bodies were destroyed),
score 100 for every adventurer, large XP and gold pools shared out at the next town, and the party stays on Olympus.
Each version still shows its own ending screens.

For the DOS ending (DIVINE I and back to Pelnor), set Options → I → AFTER THE BLACK LORD to BACK TO PELNOR, or pick
the Original (DOS) preset, before starting a new game. The choice is locked per save. Saves from earlier versions follow
their preset: Original (DOS) keeps the DOS ending, the other presets get the new one.

## Fixed

- The Apple II party wipe now reads "ARRIVED" (the original's typo is fixed).
- If an art file in your copy is damaged, the import warns and keeps the game playable without that art instead of
  failing.
- The Apple II Phantasie I's two misspelt monster names are fixed.

## Tested

The new imports, art, sounds and endings were played on Linux. The Windows and macOS downloads are built by CI and run
the same code.
